# Additional Products (SAP LeanIX add-ons) — LeanIX vs VantageMap

**Scope note.** This 135-page SAP Help doc covers the three *Additional Products* that extend LeanIX Application Portfolio Management: (1) **SAP AI Agent Hub** (pp. 2–31) — vendor-agnostic discovery and governance of AI agents, MCP servers, and LLMs; (2) **SAP LeanIX Technology Risk and Compliance** (pp. 31–59) — infrastructure-layer discovery, obsolescence-risk management, and technology-standards management; (3) **SAP LeanIX Architecture and Road Map Planning** (formerly BTM, pp. 60–135) — target-state modelling via Transformations, Impacts, Milestones, and the Transformations Explorer. Almost the entire document describes *paid add-on* products that sit on top of the base APM tool. Two of the three (AI Agent Hub and Architecture/Road Map Planning) map almost entirely onto VantageMap PLANV4 deferrals; Technology Risk and Compliance overlaps most with what VantageMap already ships.

## Key gaps

Focusing on **core EA-tool parity** (i.e. excluding the large PLANV4-deferred AI Agent Hub and Architecture/Road Map Planning add-ons):

- **Initiative Milestones (#33)** — no first-class milestone entity. The Roadmap page references milestones cosmetically but the data model can't store go-live/decision dates as discrete, referenceable markers. This is the single most impactful non-deferred gap for roadmap credibility.
- **Roadmap dependency lines + richer roadmap report (#34)** — Requires/Required-by and Blocks/Blocked-by relationship rendering on the Gantt; VantageMap has the relationship types (`requires`/`required by` in `enums.ts`) but doesn't visualise them on the timeline.
- **Application-level obsolescence risk aggregation & percentage views (#17–20)** — VantageMap computes per-component risk levels but does not roll risk up to applications through the relationship graph, lacks the Risk-Accepted/Addressed override, and lacks Missing-Data / Mitigated / Unaddressed percentage views and a dedicated obsolescence dashboard.
- **Verification vs Quality Seal (#3)** — LeanIX treats "verified for use" as an independent governance decision separate from data-quality; VantageMap conflates this into the Quality Seal. Adding a distinct verification/approval flag would close a governance-semantics gap.
- **Configurable / entity-specific reports & dashboards (#4, #5, #19, #24)** — VantageMap's reports and dashboard are a fixed set; LeanIX ships report *collections* and *configurable report types* (landscape/matrix/portfolio) plus custom KPI dashboards. This is the recurring "configurable inventory/report/view builder" gap.
- **Distinct vendor vs internal lifecycle fields (#16)** — a small but real gap: VantageMap has one lifecycle + EOL/EOS dates, LeanIX separates catalog-sourced vendor lifecycle from internal timelines.

## Equivalence summary

For the **base EA capabilities this doc touches**, VantageMap is close: it already carries the exact AI-agent meta-model subtypes (AI Agent / MCP Server / AI Model), a full Technology Radar with Adopt/Trial/Assess/Hold + technical-standard fields (near-identical to LeanIX Technology Standards Management), IT-component EOL/EOS lifecycle data, an obsolescence-risk report, tags, and import/export — so the *inventory + tech-standards + basic-obsolescence* core is largely EQUIVALENT or a short hop away. However, the doc is dominated by three **paid add-on products**, and the two heaviest — SAP AI Agent Hub (discovery, verification records, business-value calculations, agent portal, telemetry) and Architecture & Road Map Planning (transformations, impacts, transformation explorer, scenario comparison, time-slider projections) — map almost entirely onto VantageMap's PLANV4 deferrals (15.2, 15.4, 15.5, 15.6, 16.3). The genuine, non-deferred core gaps are concentrated in **roadmap depth** (milestones, dependency lines) and **obsolescence-risk maturity** (application-level aggregation, percentage views, dedicated dashboard), plus the platform-wide theme of **configurable reports/dashboards** that recurs across every LeanIX doc.
