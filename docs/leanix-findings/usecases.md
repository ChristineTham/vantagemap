# Use Cases and Methodologies — LeanIX vs VantageMap

**Scope note.** This 147-page SAP LeanIX document is the *Use Cases and Methodologies* guide. It walks through the end-to-end EA methodologies LeanIX supports: Application Portfolio Assessment (pp.2–33), Application Rationalization (pp.34–59), Application Modernization (pp.60–82), Obsolescence Risk Management (pp.83–103), SAP ERP Transformation with Integrated Toolchain (pp.104–105), AI Governance (pp.106–123), and the three portfolio-assessment frameworks — Gartner TIME, 6R, and Pace Layering (pp.123–147). It is a *workflow* document rather than a feature-reference: it repeatedly names the underlying LeanIX capabilities (fact sheets, meta model, relations, subscriptions, quality seal, mandatory attributes, completion score, surveys, to-dos, tags, saved searches, reports/views, dashboards, KPIs, presentations, diagrams, calculations, transformations, decisions, cost/TCO, reference catalog, integrations/discovery, automations, portals). Each of those is classified below. VantageMap already implements the *analytical spine* of these use cases (TIME, 6R, capability/org modelling, lifecycle, health, fixed reports) but lacks the LeanIX data-collection, cost, calculation, and target-state machinery.

## Key gaps

Core EA-tool parity gaps this document surfaces (excluding deferred transformation/AI/connector items):

- **Configurable reports / view builder (rows 24–30, 32–33).** LeanIX's use cases lean heavily on *configurable* Landscape, Matrix, Portfolio, and Roadmap reports (cluster-by, left/right property, view selector, rolled-up layout, circle-size, time slider) and user-composed dashboards with KPI cards. VantageMap has a strong but *fixed* set of reports and a fixed dashboard — this is the single largest functional gap for these workflows.
- **Cost / TCO management (row 23).** Application rationalization and the exec dashboard are built around TCO (license/maintenance/support) and cost allocation to capabilities/orgs. VantageMap has no cost fields or allocation logic at all.
- **Calculations engine (row 22).** LeanIX auto-derives TIME/6R (and AI business value) from fit fields and keeps them in sync. VantageMap stores TIME/6R as manual enums with no recompute.
- **Obsolescence risk roll-up (rows 39–40).** VantageMap reports EOL/EOS at the component and app level but does not aggregate IT-component obsolescence risk *onto related applications* via relations, and has no obsolescence-risk-status on relations / risk-dispensation modelling — the heart of the Obsolescence Risk use case.
- **Data-collection & governance depth (rows 8–13).** Survey campaigns (send/target-by-role/remind/auto-populate), mandatory-attribute gating, configurable quality-seal renewal, per-fact-sheet weighted completion score, and named subscription roles / contacts are all partial or missing — these drive LeanIX's "collect and maintain data" methodology.
- **Presentations, diagrams, decisions, file attachments, milestones, contracts (rows 35–37, 41, 44, 49, 56).** A cluster of supporting features referenced across every use case that VantageMap lacks.

## Equivalence summary

For this doc's domain, VantageMap covers the **conceptual and analytical core** of LeanIX's flagship use cases very well: the 12-type meta model, capability/org hierarchies, relations, lifecycle/health, functional/technical fit, business criticality, and the TIME, 6R, and tech-radar frameworks are all present and faithfully modelled in `src/db/schema/enums.ts` and the corresponding pages. Where it falls short is the **operational machinery** LeanIX wraps around those concepts — configurable report/dashboard builders, a cost/TCO layer, a calculations engine, obsolescence-risk roll-up through relations, and the deeper data-collection governance loop (survey campaigns, mandatory attributes, completion scoring). Roughly speaking VantageMap delivers the "what" of these methodologies but only a fixed slice of the "how-to-explore-and-report", so it reads as a solid MVP baseline that is EQUIVALENT on data model but PARTIAL/MISSING on the reporting-configurability and cost dimensions; the transformation-scenario, AI-governance, connector/discovery, and portal portions are correctly DEFERRED to PLANV4.
